Solitaire: A Simple One

Simple versions of the games you already love. No daily rewards, no streaks, no energy timers, no guilt. Just the game.

Four classic solitaire games in one simple app: Klondike, FreeCell, Spider and TriPeaks. No account, no daily rewards, no guilt.

Play your way

  • Play when you want. A game takes a few minutes and nothing is waiting for you tomorrow.
  • Deals you can trust. Klondike Easy, Medium and Hard deals and random FreeCell deals are checked by a solver, so they can be won. Spider with 4 suits is not promised, and we say so.
  • Every deal is numbered, so you can replay one you liked. FreeCell has the classic deals, #1 to #1,000,000.
  • Unlimited undo and 3 hints per game when you get stuck. In FreeCell a solver produces them.
  • In Klondike and FreeCell, once every card is face up, one tap finishes the game for you.
  • Portrait or landscape, on phone or tablet.

The games

  • Klondike: Draw 1 or Draw 3, chosen before every game. Easy, Medium, Hard or random deals.
  • FreeCell: eight columns, four cells, every card in view.
  • Spider: 1, 2 or 4 suits.
  • TriPeaks: clear three peaks with cards one higher or one lower.

Track your progress

  • Statistics for each game: games played and won, win rate, best time, fewest moves, and your current and best winning streak.
  • Pick up an unfinished game right where you left it.

Make it yours

  • Mix and match card decks and table backgrounds. Classic and Gradient are included, with more in the Shop.
  • Deck editor: turn your own photos into cards. Replace a single card, a whole suit, the full deck or the card back, and set any picture as your table.
  • Your pictures stay on your device. They are never uploaded or shared.

What you won't find here

  • Daily-login rewards or daily streaks to protect
  • Energy or lives timers
  • Notifications asking you to come back
  • An account to create
  • Betting or real-money play
  • Ads in the middle of a game

About ads, honestly

Short ads can appear between games, never during one. The first few games have none, and after that no more than one ad every two games. Watching an ad for an extra hint is always optional. A one-time purchase removes ads for good.

Simple and private

  • No account: just open and play.
  • Plays offline.
  • Available in English, Spanish, Italian, German and French.
